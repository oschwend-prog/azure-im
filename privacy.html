Who we are

azure IM Ltd. ("azure IM", "we") is a company registered in England and Wales, with its registered office at 19 Queen's Gate, London SW7 5JE. We are the controller of the personal data described in this notice. You can contact us about privacy at info@azure-im.com.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us:

  • your name, email address and message when you use the contact form;
  • the content of any email you send to us.

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not set cookies of its own.

Why we use it

We use your details to read and reply to your enquiry and, where it leads to a discussion, to keep a record of that correspondence. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running our business. Where you are contacting us on behalf of a company, we process your business contact details on the same basis.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. We use a small number of service providers to run this website and deliver messages:

  • Vercel Inc. hosts this website and the contact form;
  • Resend delivers contact-form messages to our inbox;
  • our email provider stores our correspondence.

When you load this site, your browser also requests fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries and images from public content delivery networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr, Unsplash and Pixabay). These providers receive your IP address as part of the request. Some of these providers are based outside the UK; where they are, transfers rely on UK adequacy regulations or appropriate contractual safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them. If no ongoing relationship follows, we delete them within 24 months.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to your personal data, and to ask us to correct, delete or restrict it, or to object to our use of it. To make a request, email info@azure-im.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
